The Night Shift at the Abandoned Hospital

Some night shifts are quiet. Some are unforgettable. And some should never have been taken.

Jessica was a nursing student looking for extra money. When she was offered $300 for a single overnight security shift at an abandoned hospital, she thought it would be easy money.

The hospital was called St. Agnes Mercy Hospital. It had been closed for eleven years after a devastating fire on the third floor. Seven patients died that night.

Instructions: Stay at reception. Do not go to the third floor. If alarm sounds, call supervisor.

11:47 PM - Camera 4 showed third-floor hallway. A wheelchair was moving slowly by itself. Nobody pushing it.

12:13 AM - PA system crackled. Woman's voice: "Dr. Parker, please report to Ward 3B." The PA was supposed to be dead for 11 years.

12:31 AM - Wheelchair appeared again. This time someone was sitting in it. A woman with burned gown, head hanging, long black hair.

1:09 AM - Footsteps from stairs. Three knocks. Little girl: "Nurse? My medicine is over." Then PA cried: "She took my baby..." All 12 CCTV went black. When back, Camera 1 showed Jessica at reception, but a burned woman stood behind her, hands on her shoulders. Jessica turned - nothing. She ran, leaving keys, phone, $300.

The Next Morning

Security said: According to log, you left at 1:11 AM. But cameras show you sitting till 6:00 AM, talking to someone.

The Truth About Ward 3B

Ward 3B was a maternity ward. During fire, a baby was left in incubator. Never found.

Some doors are closed for a reason. And some floors should never be visited after midnight.
