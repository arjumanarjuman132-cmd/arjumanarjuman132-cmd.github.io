THE HAUNTED HOSTEL - ROOM 417

Maya had always believed that old buildings made strange noises. Pipes rattled. Wooden floors creaked. Windows shook when the wind became strong. So when she moved into the oldest hostel on campus, she wasn't worried.

The hostel was called Westbridge Hall, a four-story-looking building that had actually been built with only three usable floors. Its walls were yellow with age, the corridors smelled of damp wood, and most of the furniture looked like it had been there longer than the students. Maya was assigned Room 214. Her roommate, Lily, was supposed to share the room with her. But Lily left after only three nights. Before leaving, she packed her belongings into two bags and stood beside the door. “Maya,” she said quietly, “there's something you need to know. If you hear noises at night, ignore them. Anything. And don't leave your shoes outside the room after midnight.” Maya laughed. “That sounds incredibly specific.” Lily didn't laugh. “I'm serious.” Then she left.

THE FOOTPRINTS

3:00 AM - Fourth Night
Scrape… scrape… scrape. Something sounded as if it was being dragged across the corridor floor. Maya remained in bed. The sound stopped outside her door. Then came silence.

The next morning, she opened her door and froze. Her shoes were sitting outside. She was certain she had left them inside. But that wasn't the frightening part. The floor around them was covered with muddy footprints. The footprints started beside her shoes and continued down the hallway. They passed the staircase. They passed the common room. Then they stopped at the very end of the corridor. There was a blank wall there. No door. No window. Nothing. They ended directly against the wall. She called the hostel cleaner and asked if someone had walked through the corridor during the night. The cleaner shook his head. “No one goes there after midnight. Because there's nowhere to go.”

THE RECORDING

That evening, Maya decided to prove to herself that someone was playing a prank. She placed her shoes outside her room. Then she positioned her phone on the floor, pointing toward the corridor. She started recording. At 2:13 AM, Maya woke suddenly. She heard footsteps. Slow footsteps. Step… Step… Step… She looked toward the door. Nothing. When the sun came up, she remembered the phone. For several minutes, nothing happened. Then, at exactly 2:13 AM, the corridor lights flickered. A girl appeared at the far end. The girl was wearing an old-fashioned hostel uniform. Her hair covered most of her face. She walked slowly toward Maya's room. But something was wrong. Her feet weren't touching the floor. The girl continued walking until she reached the camera. Then she turned her head toward the blank wall at the end of the corridor. She raised one hand. And pointed. For the first time, she noticed something behind the girl. A door. A dark wooden door covered in dust. A door that didn't exist in the real hallway.

THE OLD ROOM

Maya went straight to the warden. The moment she showed the recording, the woman's expression changed. “That door hasn't existed for twenty years.” Twenty years earlier, Westbridge Hall had a fourth floor. Twelve students lived there. One of them was a girl named Elena Ward. Elena disappeared during the winter semester. The official report said she had left the hostel voluntarily. But the students who lived with her told a different story. They claimed Elena had become obsessed with a locked room at the end of the hallway. She said she could hear someone moving furniture inside it. Nobody believed her. Then one morning, Elena was gone. After that, the fourth floor was sealed. The university eventually removed the staircase and renovated the building. The fourth floor disappeared from the official plans. But the old room remained.

ROOM 417

Maya should have moved out. Instead, she became obsessed. She searched the university archives. She found an old photograph of Westbridge Hall. The photograph showed the building before the renovation. There was a fourth floor. And at the end of that floor was the same wooden door from her recording. Someone was standing in front of the door. It was Elena. Written above the door was a room number: 417. That night her shoes were gone again. The corridor was empty. Muddy footprints led toward the end of the hallway. The lights went out. A cold breeze moved through the corridor. When the emergency light flickered back on, Maya saw something that made her unable to move. A wooden door stood where the wall had been. 417. Inside was a bedroom covered in dust. An old bed stood against the wall. A broken wardrobe leaned in the corner. On the desk sat a small music box. The music box suddenly began playing. A photograph lay beside it. It showed Elena standing with eleven other students. Then Maya noticed the date. The photograph had been taken exactly twenty years ago. She turned it over. Written on the back were twelve names. Eleven had been crossed out. Elena's name remained. Underneath it was another name. Maya Carter. Then someone whispered from behind her. “Don't turn around.” She slowly looked at the mirror hanging beside the wardrobe. The room behind her was empty. But in the mirror… Elena was standing directly behind her. Maya screamed and ran. When she looked back, the wooden door had disappeared. Only the old wall remained.

THE FINAL RECORDING

Maya left Westbridge Hall the next morning. She never returned. The university later investigated the second-floor corridor. They found nothing. No hidden room. No fourth floor. No evidence that Room 417 had ever existed. But Maya kept the recording from that night. Months later, she finally watched it again. The camera had continued recording after she ran away. For several minutes, the empty corridor remained completely silent. Then a shadow appeared. A girl walked toward the camera. Elena. She stopped directly in front of the lens. Then she slowly moved aside. Behind her was Room 417. The door was open. And someone was standing inside. It wasn't Elena. It was Maya. Wearing the same clothes she had worn that night. The recording suddenly ended. Maya deleted the video. But the next morning, she found her old hostel shoes outside her apartment door. They were covered in fresh mud. And beside them was a small piece of paper. On it was a room number.

417.
