THE NIGHT SHIFT AT THE ABANDONED HOSPITAL

Some night shifts are quiet. Some are unforgettable. And some should never have been taken.

Jessica was a nursing student looking for a way to earn some extra money. When she was offered $300 for a single overnight security shift at an abandoned hospital, she thought it would be easy.

The hospital was called St. Agnes Mercy Hospital. It had been closed for eleven years after a devastating fire on the third floor. Seven patients died that night.

Jessica's instructions were simple: Stay at reception.
Do not go to the third floor.
If an alarm sounds, call the supervisor.

At 10:50 PM, Jessica arrived at the hospital.

The building was completely dark. Before leaving, the security guard gave her the keys and warned her about one strange thing. The hospital's old PA system had been disconnected for eleven years. Yet sometimes, it still made announcements. Jessica laughed. The guard didn't.

11:47 PM

Jessica was sitting at reception, watching the CCTV cameras, when something caught her attention. Camera 4 showed the third-floor hallway. A wheelchair was moving slowly through the darkness. There was nobody pushing it.

12:13 AM

The PA system suddenly crackled above her. Then a woman's voice calmly spoke through the old speakers. "Dr. Parker, please report to Ward 3B." Jessica froze. The PA system was supposed to be dead.

12:31 AM

The wheelchair appeared again on Camera 4. This time, someone was sitting in it. A woman. Her head was hanging down, and long black hair covered her face. She was wearing a burned patient gown.

1:09 AM

Jessica heard footsteps coming from the hospital stairs. Someone was walking down. Slowly. The footsteps stopped outside the reception door. Then came three quiet knocks. Knock. Knock. Knock. A little girl's voice came from the other side. "Nurse? My medicine is over. Can you come?"

Then the PA system crackled again. This time, the woman's voice was crying. "She took my baby..."

Jessica looked toward the CCTV monitor. Suddenly, all twelve screens went black. For two seconds, there was complete darkness. Then the cameras came back. Camera 1 showed the reception desk. Jessica was sitting in her chair. But something was standing behind her. A burned woman. Her hands were resting on Jessica's shoulders. Jessica turned around. There was nothing there. She ran. She left the keys, the phone, and the $300 behind.

The Next Morning

The security company called her. They asked why she had left the building. Jessica told them what happened. Then they said something that made her blood run cold. According to the CCTV footage, Jessica had left the hospital at 1:11 AM. But the cameras showed her sitting at reception until 6:00 AM. And during those five hours... She appeared to be talking to someone.

The Truth About Ward 3B

Later, Jessica discovered something she had never been told. St. Agnes Mercy Hospital really had a Ward 3B. It was a maternity ward. And eleven years earlier, during the fire, a baby had been left inside an incubator. The baby was never found. Neither was the truth about what happened that night.

Some people say the woman on the CCTV was a patient. Others believe she was searching for her missing baby. But Jessica knows one thing. She will never work another night shift again. Because sometimes... the hospital isn't as abandoned as everyone thinks.

Some doors are closed for a reason. And some floors should never be visited after midnight.
