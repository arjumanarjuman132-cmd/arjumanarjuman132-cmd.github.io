Don't Look At Me - The Girl In My Ohio Apartment Closet

True Horror Story | Columbus, Ohio, USA | By Scary Story Vault

I never believed in cheap rent being a red flag. I should have.

Last August, I moved to Columbus, Ohio for a job. I found a listing on Craigslist - a studio apartment near campus for $550 a month. In that area, everything is like $900 minimum. I called the number immediately.

An old man picked up. He said his name was Mr. Henderson. He told me I could come see it today. The building was old, paint peeling, but the apartment itself was clean. Too clean.

He gave me the keys and then he said it. He looked right at the bedroom closet and said, "One rule. After midnight, if you hear anything from in there, don't look. Just go to sleep. Don't open it."

I laughed a little. I thought he was one of those weird old landlords who likes to scare the new kid. I said okay, sure.

The first week was normal. The closet was just a closet. I put my clothes in there, my shoes. Nothing weird.

It started on a Tuesday. I remember because I had an early shift on Wednesday. I was asleep and I woke up around 2:47 AM. I checked my phone for the time. The room was silent, but my closet door was open. About an inch. I always close it fully because the light from the hallway comes through the crack and it bothers me.

I got up and closed it. Went back to sleep.

The next night, same thing. Open an inch. This time I heard something. Like someone breathing. Very slow, very quiet. I told myself it was the AC vent. That closet has an old vent in it.

On the third night, I heard her.

I was lying in bed, trying to sleep, closet door open again. And then a little girl's voice. So clear, like she was standing right inside the closet.

"Don't look at me."

I froze. My whole body went cold. I thought maybe a kid from another apartment was playing a prank? But it was 3 AM.

I whispered, "Who is there?"

No answer for like ten seconds. Then she said it again, louder this time, almost angry.

"Don't... look... at... me!"

My heart was beating so fast I could hear it. I wanted to run out of the apartment. I wanted to call Mr. Henderson. But something in me, that stupid human curiosity, made me get up.

I slowly walked to the closet. My hand was shaking on the handle. I opened it.

And there was a girl sitting on the floor. Her back was to me. She had long, black, messy hair that covered her whole back. She was wearing a white dress that was dirty, like it had been in that closet for years. She was very small, maybe 7 or 8 years old.

She was shaking.

I said, "Hey... are you okay? How did you get in here?"

She didn't turn. She just whispered, "You looked. I told you not to look. You weren't supposed to look."

And then she started to turn around. Slowly.

I will never forget what I saw. Or what I didn't see. Her face... there was no face. It was just smooth skin. No eyes. No nose. No mouth. Just blank skin stretched over her skull. But I could still hear her voice.

She stood up. Her arms were too long. She reached out and touched my cheek with her cold fingers.

And everything went black.

I woke up on my bedroom floor the next morning. Sun was out. My closet was closed. But it was locked from the outside with a small padlock that was never there before.

I called Mr. Henderson. He came over in like 20 minutes. He didn't look surprised at all. He said, "She showed herself to you, didn't she?"

He said the previous tenant, a single mom and her daughter, lived there three years ago. The little girl, Emily, loved to play hide and seek in that closet. One day she was hiding and the mom accidentally locked the closet from the outside and went to work. She forgot. Emily was in there for two days.

Since then, every person who rents this apartment hears her at night.

I moved out that same day. I lost my $550 but I didn't care.

Last week I got a call from a new number. It was a young guy. He said, "Hey, you lived in apartment 3B before?" Before I could answer, I heard a little girl's voice in the background of his call, whispering.

"Don't look at me."

I hung up. And I swear, last night, I heard scratching from inside my new apartment's closet.
