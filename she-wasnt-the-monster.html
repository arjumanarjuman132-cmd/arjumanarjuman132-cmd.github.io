SHE WASN'T THE MONSTER

True Horror Story | Forest Horror | By Scary Story Vault

She thought the witch was the most terrifying thing in the forest.

She was wrong.

Late one night, a young woman gets stranded on a dark forest road. With no phone signal and nowhere to go, she begins walking through the trees.

Then she hears a voice.

“Help me…”

She follows the sound until she sees a mysterious woman standing between the trees.

A witch.

The witch looks at her and says only one thing:

“Don’t answer her.”

But before she can understand the warning, a stranger arrives in a car and offers her a way out.

She gets inside.

Soon, she realizes something is wrong.

The driver knows about the witch.

The road suddenly changes.

The radio begins sending a strange warning.

And when she looks at the driver's reflection…

it doesn't move.

She escapes and finds an old cabin hidden deep inside the forest. Inside, she discovers photographs of people who disappeared.

Then she finds a journal.

The truth begins to reveal itself.

The driver was once human.

The forest took him—and now he collects people who enter it.

But the witch wasn't trying to hurt her.

She was trying to protect her.

The girl reaches two doors.

One leads back to the forest.

The other leads to something much worse.

She chooses the wrong one.

Suddenly, she finds herself in a darker version of the forest. Pale faces appear between the trees. The driver is waiting.

The witch appears and screams:

“RUN!”

Then she reveals the truth.

The driver cannot leave the forest unless someone takes his place.

The witch pushes the girl through the doorway and saves her.

She wakes up alone on the forest road.

For a moment, everything seems normal.

Then she looks at her wrist.

A dark handprint has appeared.

A whisper comes from behind her:

“Don’t let him find you again.”

She turns around.

Nothing is there.

But deep inside the forest…

the witch and the driver are still waiting.

THE END. 👻
