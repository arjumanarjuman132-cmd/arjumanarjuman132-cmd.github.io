THE HAUNTED HOSTEL - ROOM 417

Maya had always believed that old buildings made strange noises. She had no idea how important a warning would become.

Maya was assigned Room 214 at Westbridge Hall, the oldest hostel on campus. Its walls were yellow with age, the corridors smelled of damp wood. Her roommate, Lily, was supposed to share the room with her. But Lily left after only three nights.

Before leaving, she stood beside the door. “Maya,” she said quietly, “there's something you need to know. If you hear noises at night, ignore them. Anything. And don't leave your shoes outside the room after midnight.”

THE FOOTPRINTS

3:00 AM - Fourth Night Maya woke up because of a strange sound. Scrape… scrape… scrape. Something was being dragged across the corridor floor. It stopped outside her door. Then silence.

The next morning, she opened her door and froze. Her shoes were sitting outside. She was certain she had left them inside. But the floor around them was covered with muddy footprints. They started beside her shoes and continued down the hallway. They passed the staircase, passed the common room, then stopped at the very end of the corridor. There was a blank wall there. No door. No window. The footprints ended directly against the wall.

The cleaner said: “No one goes there after midnight. Because there's nowhere to go.”

THE RECORDING

That evening, Maya placed her shoes outside and positioned her phone on the floor, recording the corridor.

At 2:13 AM, the corridor lights flickered. A girl appeared at the far end. She was wearing an old-fashioned hostel uniform. Her hair covered most of her face.
